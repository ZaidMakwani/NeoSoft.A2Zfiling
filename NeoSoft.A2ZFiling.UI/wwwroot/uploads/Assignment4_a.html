Welcome to SIES college

SIES College of Arts, Science & Commerce, situated in Sion (West), Mumbai, is a prestigious educational institution. It offers a diverse range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs. Notable undergraduate courses include B.A., B.M.S, B.Sc, and B.A.M.M.C. The college also provides postgraduate programs in M.Sc and other fields. Achievements include NAAC re-accreditation, innovation initiatives, and quality awards. The college boasts star alumni such as Shankar Mahadevan, Shreya Ghoshal, and Suma Shirur. For more information, visit the official website. The college has a rich history dating back to its establishment in 1960 by Shri Y. B. Chavan, the first Chief Minister of Maharashtra .
