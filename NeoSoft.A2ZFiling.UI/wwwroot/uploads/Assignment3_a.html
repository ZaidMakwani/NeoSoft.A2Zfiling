Explore Nepal

Pokhara

Pokhara is known for its stunning views of the Annapurna range and serene Phewa Lake. It's a gateway to the treks in the Annapurna area.

Kathmandu

The capital city, Kathmandu, is rich in historical and cultural heritage, featuring many temples and palaces.

Lumbini

Lumbini is the birthplace of Lord Buddha and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, attracting pilgrims from around the world.
